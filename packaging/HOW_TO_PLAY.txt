DUNGEON SANDBOX - HOW TO PLAY
=============================

An original third-person dungeon game in the style of classic tab-target MMO
dungeons. You play one character; four AI companions (or nine, in a raid
group) fill the tank, healer and damage roles. Everything here - names, maps,
numbers, art - is original and made of simple shapes.

STARTING THE GAME
-----------------
1. Unzip the whole folder somewhere you can write to (e.g. Desktop or
   Documents). Do not run it from inside the ZIP.
2. Double-click dungeon-sandbox.exe.
3. Windows SmartScreen may warn about an unrecognised app because the build
   is not code-signed. Choose "More info" -> "Run anyway" only if you got the
   ZIP from someone you trust.

Your progress is saved in the "saves" folder next to the exe (created on
first save). Delete that folder to start over. To keep saves elsewhere, set
the environment variable DUNGEON_SAVE_DIR to a folder path.

Requirements: 64-bit Windows 10/11 and a graphics card with DirectX 12 or
Vulkan support. Integrated graphics work.

THE LOOP
--------
Hub -> recruit companions and pick their AI style -> pick a dungeon and a
difficulty -> walk to the portal and press E -> fight through the dungeon ->
loot and XP -> back to the hub (Enter on the results screen).

In the hub, click the panel tabs (or press 1-8): Party, Character, Dungeons,
Classes, History, Sandbox, Vendor and Editor. Hover anything for a tooltip.

CONTROLS
--------
  W / S              move forward / back (moving cancels casts)
  A / D              turn (strafe while holding the right mouse button)
  Q / E              strafe
  Right mouse drag   turn camera and character
  Left mouse drag    look around; a short click selects
  Both mouse buttons run forward
  Mouse wheel        zoom
  V                  camera auto-follow on/off

  Tab / Shift+Tab    cycle enemies
  Click              select any character or party frame
  F1-F5              select a party member
  T                  target of target
  G                  the party's kill target
  1-5                your class abilities (heals go to the ally under the
                     mouse, else your selected ally, else you)
  F                  auto-attack
  Esc                clear target
  L                  detailed combat log
  K                  your numbers for this fight on/off
  N / Shift+N        navigation overlay (debug)
  Backspace          leave the dungeon (counts as a failed run)
  E                  (hub) enter the selected dungeon at the portal
  F5                 (hub) reload dungeon files

CLASSES
-------
  Warden   - tank: hold enemies' attention (threat), taunt, interrupt
  Mender   - healer: heals, heal-over-time, absorb shield, dispel
  Striker  - melee damage: fight from behind, interrupt
  Arcanist - ranged damage: crowd control (Hex), interrupt

READING THE FIGHT
-----------------
- Above the action bar, on the right: what your last abilities did
  ("Shield Slam > Ghoul: 82 CRIT", "Kick > interrupted ...",
  "Mend > Brannoc: +120 (300 overheal)").
- Your floating numbers are the large ones; companions' are small and
  pale.
- Enemy nameplate colours show threat: red means it is attacking you.
- Orange/red rings on the ground are telegraphs: step out before they fill.
- Cast bars on enemies can often be interrupted.
- Lava is bright and glowing; raised rock lips mark its edge. Orange strips on
  ledges mark one-way drops.
- Walls and floors block line of sight: "no line of sight" means move.

THE CINDERDEEP HOLLOWS (new in 0.5, level 5+)
---------------------------------------------
A lava cavern descent, about 8-15 minutes on a first clear:
- Frostvent Gate: a cool, blue-lit entrance guarded by scouts.
- Glowworm Fork: choose the low Ashgrub Run (optional den) or the high Ridge
  Path (optional hounds) - the ridge ends in a one-way drop into the Smelter.
- Smelter Gallery: Kilnmaster Vorsk fights beside a slag pit. Spread out when
  the slag spills.
- Obsidian Grotto (optional): the Glass Matriarch - interrupt her Glass Nova.
- Kiln Span: a narrow bridge over a lava river, held by shield-bearers with
  frontal cleaves guarding a caster. Don't stand in front of them.
- Magma Cistern: a brute and imps on a basalt island between lava channels,
  with fire vents.
- Heart Island: Ignarax, the Molten Heart. Three phases: eruptions and spawn
  from the moat at 60%, a relentless Heart Pulse at 25%.

THE EMBERFORGE VAULTS (new in 0.6, level 3+)
--------------------------------------------
Built by Dungeon Forge, the game's dungeon generator, from a blueprint
rather than by hand: Emberkin raiders, a guarded caster, Forgewarden Hask
beside a lava pit and Cindermaw, the Furnace Wyrm, in a braziered arena.
Look for the second route between two rooms, a raised ledge that drops (one
way) further in, a bridge over lava and an optional elite in a side cell.
In 0.7 the caverns are irregular, longer passages wind between them, and a
pack of ash hounds patrols one hall: watch its route before you pull.

THE HOLLOW RELIQUARY (new in 0.7, level 4+)
-------------------------------------------
A second Dungeon Forge dungeon, from a different kit: the buried monastery of
the Hushed. Narrow doors open into long naves with solid stone pillars that
block movement and line of sight. Fights to expect:
- Warden's Screen: a shield-bearer with a frontal cleave guarding two healers.
  Get round him and interrupt the heals.
- Sentinel Patrol: two halberd sentinels pacing a nave.
- Bone Archers: they shoot across the hall. Pull them round a pillar so they
  have to come to you.
- The Twin Abbots: one heals, the other chants. Split your interrupts and
  pick a kill order.
- The Hollow Guardian: when it begins to wail, get a pillar between you and
  it. The wail only hits those it can see.
Your companions take cover from the wail on their own.

Difficulty: Normal, Heroic (harder, better loot) and Raid (10). The Sandbox tab
can make any dungeon easier or harder without editing files.

CLASS DESIGN LAB (experiment, 0.10): the Sandbox tab can switch your Striker
to one of two experimental styles for this session only (never saved):
  A: Planned  - build Edge (+2 from behind), spend it on a bleed or a whirl.
  B: Reactive - Exploit openings: enemies casting, just after their
                abilities, after your Kick, or below 30% health.
See PLAYTEST_CLASS_LAB_0_10.md in the repository for the playtest.

DUNGEON VIEWER (new in 0.8)
---------------------------
Look around any dungeon without fighting: double-click "Dungeon Viewer.bat",
or press V in the hub (Dungeons tab: "Inspect in viewer"). Nothing is saved.
- Hold the right mouse button to look; WASD to move, E/Q up and down,
  Shift faster, Ctrl slower, mouse wheel sets the speed.
- G walks at eye height, Tab jumps to the next room, L lists the rooms,
  Home goes back to the entrance.
- 1-4 show navigation, rooms, enemies and the building pieces; click
  something to read about it.
- [ and ] show other layouts of the generated dungeons.
- F12 takes a screenshot, Backspace returns to the hub.
In 0.8 the Emberforge Vaults and the Hollow Reliquary are built from modular
stone and basalt pieces, and the light and haze change from room to room.

DUNGEON REMIX (new in 0.9)
--------------------------
The Emberforge Vaults and the Hollow Reliquary can be played as shipped or
remixed: the same fights and bosses in different rooms, with different side
branches and approaches. In the hub's Dungeons tab, select one of them and
use the Layout row:
- Classic: the dungeon as shipped.
- Remix (new seed): a new layout; its seed is shown.
- Seed: click it, type a number, press Enter. The same seed always gives the
  same layout, so you can replay one or share it.
Branches are there for a reason. Look for:
- a glowing tablet or a bright lamp beside a doorway: something is through
  there (an optional fight, a treasure chest, or both);
- a dim candle or ember lantern: a side passage, often a way into a guarded
  hall from the side or behind;
- in the Emberforge Vaults, pulsing magma vents in a corridor: time your
  crossing, or take the longer tunnel round;
- warm light: a landmark worth a look (no loot).
Treasure chests open when you walk up to them; their loot is added at the
end of the run. Nothing optional is ever needed to finish the dungeon.
New layouts are written to a "generated" folder next to your save.

TROUBLESHOOTING
---------------
- "could not read content file": the assets folder must sit next to
  dungeon-sandbox.exe. Re-extract the whole ZIP.
- Black window or crash at start: update your graphics driver.
- Optional: dungeon-headless.exe runs simulated AI-only clears from a
  command prompt, e.g.  dungeon-headless.exe --dungeon cinderdeep_hollows --runs 5
